# Task Manager: User Guide

This guide is for everyone who uses the Task Manager. You do not need any technical knowledge.

Open the app here: https://rathunuu.github.io/task-manager-final/

> The first time you open the app after a while, it can take up to a minute to wake up. Just wait, it will load.

## 1. Getting in

**New here?** Click **Sign Up**, enter your name, email and a password (at least 6 characters). Or use the **Google** button to sign in with your Google account.

**Already have an account?** Enter your email and password and log in.

If something goes wrong:

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Invalid email or password" | The email or password is wrong | Try again carefully |
| "Too many failed login attempts. Try again in N minute(s)." | There were 5 wrong passwords in a row, so that email is paused for 15 minutes | Wait, then try again. Do not keep guessing |
| "This account has been deactivated. Please contact an admin." | An admin has switched your account off | Ask an admin. Your tasks are safe and come back when you are reactivated |

## 2. Projects

A **project** is a group of tasks that a team works on together. Your projects are the buttons at the top of the page. Click one to open it.

- **Create a project:** click **+ New Project**, type a name. You become its **owner**.
- **Archived projects** disappear from the buttons. This only means the project is finished or paused. Nothing is lost. Ask an admin if you need one back.

## 3. Tasks

With a project open:

1. Type the task in the **New task...** box.
2. Pick a category (Work, Personal, Urgent) and a status (To Do, In Progress, In Review, Done).
3. Click **Add Task**.

**AI Add:** type a normal sentence such as "Send the report to Priya by Friday, high priority" and click **AI Add**. The app fills in the details for you.

Other things you can do:

- **Change the status** as work moves along (To Do → In Progress → In Review → Done).
- **Search** with the **Search tasks...** box, and sort with the sort menu.
- Open a task to see or edit its details.
- **Delete a task:** only the person who created it, or a project owner, can do this.

## 4. Working with a team

Every person in a project is either an **owner** or a **member**.

| | Member | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| See the project and its tasks | Yes | Yes |
| Add, edit and finish tasks | Yes | Yes |
| Add or remove people | No | Yes |
| Edit or delete the project | No | Yes |

**Owners only:** a **Members** box appears under the project name. Type a person's email in **Add member by email...** and add them. They must already have an account. You can remove a member there too. A project always keeps at least one owner.

If you are not a member of a project, you cannot see it at all.

## 5. For admins

Admins have an **Admin Dashboard** (use **My Tasks** to come back to your own tasks).

- **Create & Assign Task:** choose a project, tick one or more people, click **Assign to ... user(s)**. They are added to the project automatically.
- **All Users:** see everyone's progress.
  - **Deactivate** switches a person's account off. They cannot log in and any open session stops. Their tasks, comments and project memberships are kept. **Reactivate** switches it back on.
  - **Make Admin / Remove Admin** changes someone's role. You cannot change your own role, and there must always be at least one admin.
- **All Projects:** **Archive** hides a project from everyone's project buttons. **Unarchive** brings it back. There is no delete button, on purpose.
- **Reports:**
  - **Project Progress** shows, for every project, how much is done (a percentage) and how many tasks are To Do, In Progress, In Review and Completed.
  - **Overdue Tasks** lists tasks whose due date has passed and which are not finished, with the project and the person responsible.

## 6. Good habits

- Mark tasks **Done** as you finish them, so the progress numbers are true.
- Give tasks a due date, so overdue ones show up in the report.
- Never share your password. If you think someone else knows it, tell an admin straight away.
- Log out on shared computers.

## 7. Getting help

Ask an admin first. If something looks broken (a blank page that does not load after two minutes, or an error that keeps coming back), tell the person who looks after the app and describe what you clicked and what you saw.
